The Challenge

Less Than Three needed a refreshed, mobile-friendly website that stood out from crowded campus singing groups and provided clear information for concert-goers and prospective auditionees.

My Approach

Interviewed target audiences, conducted 3 iterations of card sorting to architect site hierarchy, designed responsive layouts with a signature purple & black visual identity, and built key front-end components.

01. Overview & Project Background

Less Than Three A Cappella is a co-ed student a cappella ensemble at Cornell University known for modern pop and contemporary arrangements. Our four-person team designed and engineered an entirely new web experience over the Spring 2022 semester.

Three rounds of structured card sorting shaped the site's information architecture. I led the interaction design for the home-page performance carousel, individual member bio gallery, and responsive mobile navigation, then programmed and shipped the production front-end alongside my teammates through GitHub version control.

"How might we design a vibrant digital presence that showcases Less Than Three's distinct musical energy while making concert and audition info instantly accessible?"

02. Client Collaboration & Core Goals

We partnered directly with Less Than Three's student leadership and executive board to understand their vision and operational needs for the new site. Three primary priorities emerged:

  • Keep Information Fresh & Current: Easily update singing rosters, executive board positions, and semester leadership rosters.
  • Stand Out with Signature Identity: Establish a bold visual identity centered on their signature purple and black aesthetic and high-energy music culture.
  • Streamline Key User Journeys: Make concert, gig, and ticketing details transparent for fans, while clarifying requirements and timelines for prospective auditionees.

03. Target Audience & User Interviews

The primary audience is Cornell University students interested in a cappella—either looking to attend live campus concerts or auditioning to join the group. Because this community is cohesive and highly engaged with Cornell's performing arts scene, understanding their discovery habits was critical.

We conducted user interviews with prospective audience members to uncover how they seek performance information and evaluate audition opportunities. Key interview prompts explored:

  • Have you attended a Less Than Three concert? What motivated your attendance, or what barriers prevented you?
  • Where do you typically discover upcoming music events and concerts across campus?
  • When considering auditions for music groups, what specific logistical and repertoire details do you look for in advance?
  • What impression do you expect from an ensemble's official website versus their social media?

Synthesizing User Goals

From our qualitative interview findings, three core audience goals emerged to anchor the website layout:

  1. Group Ethos & Personality: Gain a genuine sense of the group's musical style, members, and team culture.
  2. Concert & Ticketing Access: Quickly find accurate dates, venue locations, and ticket links for upcoming semester shows.
  3. Audition Guidance: Understand the audition timeline, preparation requirements, vocal ranges, and callback procedures.

04. Information Architecture & Card Sorting

Working backward from audience and client goals, we mapped out all candidate content items: group history, member bios, audition FAQ, concert dates, ticketing, video recordings, and social channels.

To ensure content hierarchy was intuitive, we executed three iterative rounds of open and closed card sorting with representative students. The findings established a focused 4-page structure where each audience goal mapped cleanly to a dedicated page:

  • Home / About: Introduces the group, latest highlights, and visual performance carousel.
  • Members: Profiles current singers, vocal parts, and executive board responsibilities.
  • Concerts: Dedicated hub for upcoming semester shows, guest appearances, and past concert videos.
  • Auditions: Step-by-step guidance for prospective singers, FAQ accordions, and sign-up links.

05. Brand Identity & Visual Language

To differentiate Less Than Three from other campus ensembles, we established three core thematic pillars that guided our typography, color palette, and visual assets:

Pillar 01 · Palette

Purple & Black

Pillar 02 · Content

Pillar 03 · Energy

Excitement & Vibe

07. Layout Explorations & Client Feedback

We sketched low-fidelity layouts across desktop and mobile to test content placement before coding. When presenting our layout iterations to the client leadership, feedback was overwhelmingly positive:

  • Strong Visual Balance: The executive board loved the bold purple/black contrast, noting it gave the club much stronger personality than their previous site.
  • Concert Poster Integration: The client suggested integrating their official semester concert poster directly on the Concert page, which we incorporated into the final build.
  • Approved Video Curation: Leadership approved our curated showcase of signature performance arrangements.

10. Reflection & Engineering Takeaways

This project was an invaluable masterclass in collaborating on production code with a multi-disciplinary team via GitHub version control—managing branches, resolving merge conflicts, and conducting peer code reviews.

Crucially, it reinforced the discipline of balancing client requirements against end-user needs. By validating our assumptions through card sorting and user interviews rather than relying on guesswork, we delivered a polished digital experience that fulfilled the client's operational vision and resonated with Cornell's student community.
